# Support handbook

The team answers requests from customers by email and by chat.

## Opening hours

Requests are answered from 08:00 to 18:00 on weekdays.
On public holidays the chat is closed.

## Response times

### Email

Every email gets a first answer within one working day.

### Chat

A chat gets an answer within five minutes.
